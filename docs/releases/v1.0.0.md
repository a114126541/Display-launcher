## English

Display Launcher lets you choose an app on the main screen and open it on a second display.

1. Search launchable apps and choose a secondary display when more than one is available.
2. Keep the picker on the main screen after launch, with a **Go to desktop** button.
3. Show **Last opened** history by default, or opt in to **Now showing** status with Accessibility access on Android 11+.
4. Switch between English and Simplified Chinese, with separate desktop and transparent in-app artwork.

Minimum Android version: 8.0, with firmware support for independent secondary-display activities. Verified on FH156A17-D / RK3566 running Android 11 with Loyverse CDS 1.8 and Android Settings. Other devices and a full device reboot have not been tested. The APK is about 245 KB.

## 简体中文

Display Launcher 可以在主屏选择应用，并将它打开到副屏。

1. 搜索有启动入口的应用；有多块副屏时，可选择目标屏幕。
2. 启动后应用列表留在主屏，并提供“回到桌面”按钮。
3. 默认显示“上次打开”的记录；Android 11 及以上可选择授予无障碍权限，显示“当前显示”的应用。
4. 支持中英文切换，桌面图标和界面透明 logo 使用独立资源。

最低要求 Android 8.0，固件需要支持在副屏独立运行应用。已在 Android 11 的 FH156A17-D / RK3566 上验证 Loyverse CDS 1.8 和系统 Settings，尚未测试其他设备及整机重启。APK 约 245 KB。
